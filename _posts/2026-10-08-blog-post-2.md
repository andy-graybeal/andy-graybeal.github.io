The mainframe. Yeah. The big iron. Humming in a room that smelled like Freon and burnt dust. A couple of guys. Real basement wizards. They kept the thing alive. Wrote the spells in COBOL, fed the tapes, prayed to the gods of the vacuum tube. And if it caught fire—well, that was just part of the gig, man. A little smoke, a little panic, a lot of coffee. And when you're on hour nineteen and the goddamn thing is eating your payroll program, you've got bigger problems.

That was then. A tight little kingdom of nerds.

Now?

Now the goddamn thing is everywhere. It's in your car, your fridge, your pacemaker. It's in the goddamn lightbulbs. It's a vast, pulsing, silicon-based fungus that has spread across the entire planet, and it is hungry.

And the wizards? The basement wizards are extinct. Now we have teams. Fucking teams. Teams for the network, teams for the software, teams for the little glass rectangles everyone stares into. Teams for the accounts. A whole department of pale, twitchy people whose only job is to watch the great digital beast for signs that someone has thrown a wrench.

And the logs. Jesus. We're drowning in it. Petabytes. Exabytes. A fucking tsunami of ones and zeros. Every click, every keystroke, every digital fart is recorded, timestamped, and filed away in a billion different formats. We got a missing 27B-6 and a mess of duct-work in the walls...

So what do we do? We build a bigger shovel. We bring in the AI. The machine. We feed the mountain of garbage into the electronic maw and ask it, "Hey, what the hell is this?" A machine watching the machines that watch the machines. It's a goddamn hall of mirrors in here, and the reflection is starting to look a little green around the gills.

The whole thing is a snake eating its own tail, and we're just the bacteria living in its gut, hoping it doesn't decide to go on a diet.

And soon…

We won't even be the ones asking.

We'll just be standing here, mouths agape, watching the two AIs talk to each other in a language we can't understand. A silent, lightning-fast conversation.

And you know what they'll be saying?

"Hold my beer."
