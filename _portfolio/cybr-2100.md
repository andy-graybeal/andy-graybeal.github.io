  - Government surveillance disclosures and the ethical tension between secrecy, whistleblowing, and the public interest.

- Flock Safety Cameras
  - Automated license plate readers and questions about surveillance, data retention, and access to vehicle-location records.

- [Benz v. PHB Realty Company](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ksd-2_22-cv-02081/pdf/USCOURTS-ksd-2_22-cv-02081-0.pdf)

### The 2024 Columbus Ransomware Breach: Public Statements, Connor Goodwolf, and the Class Action

#### ⚠️ The Breach and the City's Initial Claims

- [NBC4i Investigative Report](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/investigates/mayor-ginther-shares-impact-of-columbus-data-leak-as-next-security-steps-begin/) — Covers Mayor Ginther's statements about the stolen data and the subsequent examination of its contents.
- [StateScoop Timeline Analysis](https://statescoop.com/columbus-ohio-ransomware-saga-legal-gray-areas-2024/) — Traces the ransomware attack, the city's public statements, and the disputes that followed.
- [Ohio Capital Journal Commentary](https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2024/09/03/columbus-officials-embarrass-themselves-with-lack-of-transparency-on-data-attack/) — Criticizes the city's transparency and response to questions about the exposed data.
- [WOSU Public Media Retrospective](https://www.wosu.org/2025-07-18/one-year-later-little-details-or-accountability-shown-by-columbus-officials-after-ransomware-attack) — Reviews what remained unanswered one year after the attack.

#### 🛑 The City's Lawsuit Against Connor Goodwolf

David Leroy Ross Jr., known as Connor Goodwolf, examined data released after the breach and challenged the city's description of it. **The city sued him.** This is separate from the breach victims' case below.

- [CNBC National Investigative Report](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/09/15/dark-web-expert-warned-us-hometown-about-big-hack-the-city-is-suing.html) — Examines Ross's findings, the city's lawsuit, and cybersecurity experts' reactions.
- [Ars Technica Legal Breakdown](https://arstechnica.com/security/2024/08/city-of-columbus-sues-man-after-he-discloses-severity-of-ransomware-attack/) — Describes the city's claims against Ross after he disclosed evidence about the breach's severity.
- [Malwarebytes Industry Response](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2024/09/city-of-columbus-tries-to-silence-security-researcher) — Covers criticism of the city's legal action from the cybersecurity community.
- [Freedom of the Press Foundation First Amendment Analysis](https://freedom.press/digisec/blog/city-sues-security-researcher-after-revelations-about-ransomware-attack/) — Discusses the potential effect of the lawsuit on security research and reporting.
- [WOSU Public Media: City Drops Its Lawsuit (October 2024)](https://www.wosu.org/politics-government/2024-10-25/columbus-city-attorney-drops-lawsuit-against-cybersecurity-whistleblower) — Reports that the city dismissed its lawsuit against Ross with prejudice and describes the agreement concerning discussion of the breach and protection of private data.

#### ⚖️ Breach Victims' Proposed Class Action Against the City

People whose information was allegedly exposed **sued the City of Columbus**.

##### 2024: Filing and Expansion

- [WOSU: Columbus Police Officers File Class Action (August 2024)](https://www.wosu.org/politics-government/2024-08-12/two-columbus-police-officers-file-class-action-lawsuit-against-city-after-cyber-attack-leak) — Reports the initial suit by two officers who alleged that the breach exposed sensitive personal information.
- [10TV: Lawsuit Expanded to Other Affected People (August 2024)](https://www.10tv.com/article/news/local/columbus-cyberattack-free-credit-monitoring-and-lawsuit/530-1d95f937-0fb5-45bd-942a-ffe2fbe17281) — Reports the expansion of the proposed class action beyond the officers. Two cases filed in August were later consolidated.

##### 2025: Trial Court Dismissal

- [WOSU: Judge Dismisses the Class Action (October 2025)](https://www.wosu.org/politics-government/2025-10-01/judge-dismisses-class-action-lawsuit-against-city-of-columbus-for-2024-data-hack) — Reports the trial court's decision that governmental immunity barred the claims against the city.

##### 2026: Dismissal Reversed on Appeal

- [Doe v. Columbus — Ohio Tenth District Court of Appeals (September 24, 2026)](https://www.supremecourt.ohio.gov/rod/docs/pdf/10/2026/2026-Ohio-3743.pdf) — The appeals court reversed the dismissal and sent the case back to the trial court. It found that immunity could not be decided against the plaintiffs solely from the allegations in their complaint.
- [Columbus Dispatch: Class Action Will Move Forward (September 29, 2026)](https://bluewaterhealthyliving.com/news/national-news/ohio/class-action-cyberattack-lawsuit-against-columbus-will-move-forward/) — Summarizes the appeals ruling and the plaintiffs' allegations.

**Current status as of October 7, 2026:** The proposed class action has been returned to the trial court for further proceedings. The appeals ruling did not certify a class or determine that the city was negligent or liable.
