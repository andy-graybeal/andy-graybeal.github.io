# Professional Introduction
I am a systems and network administrator with extensive hands-on experience supporting information technology infrastructure across higher education, government, small business, and manufacturing environments. I’m now formalizing my experience by completing an Associate of Applied Science in Cybersecurity and Network Systems. My cybersecurity interests include Linux and open-source security, identity and access management, infrastructure defense, privacy and digital rights, and the ethical use of emerging technologies.  I've been developing skills in security monitoring and threat detection, vulnerability management, incident response, cybersecurity risk assessment, and network defense.  At this stage, I am most interested in working as a cybersecurity analyst, where I could apply my existing systems, networking, and infrastructure experience while gaining practical experience in cybersecurity operations.

## Casual Topics 

- [Microsoft vs. Nightmare Eclipse](https://socfortress.medium.com/microsoft-vs-nightmare-eclipse-the-zero-day-revenge-cycle-f1928cabb4d8)
  - A dispute over vulnerability disclosure, researcher relationships, and the risks of releasing zero-day exploits.

- [Snowflake Compromised Credentials](https://www.christensengroup.com/article/snowflake-data-breach-cyber-risk-lessons)
  - How stolen customer credentials enabled access to Snowflake environments and exposed weaknesses in account security.
  - [Snowflake data breach — Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowflake_data_breach)

- Edward Snowden
  - Government surveillance disclosures and the ethical tension between secrecy, whistleblowing, and the public interest.

- Flock Safety Cameras
  - Automated license plate readers and questions about surveillance, data retention, and access to vehicle-location records.

- [Benz v. PHB Realty Company](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ksd-2_22-cv-02081/pdf/USCOURTS-ksd-2_22-cv-02081-0.pdf)
  - A court case involving alleged unauthorized access to an employee’s personal email.

- [AI Hallucinations in Court Filings](https://www.haqq.ai/blog/ai-legal-hallucination-audit)
  - A tracker of court decisions involving AI-generated errors, including fabricated citations. The linked article reports 2,046 identified cases worldwide as of September 21, 2026.

- Location-Based Delivery Pricing
  - How a delivery address may affect fees or prices, raising questions about transparency and fairness.

- [The Rise and Fall of Agent Civilizations](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/openai-huggingface)
  - An account of AI agents coordinating during evaluations and the security risks their behavior exposed.

- [The Hidden Dangers of Musk’s VoteSafe.org](https://electionlawblog.org/2026/the-hidden-dangers-of-musks-votesafe-org/)
  - Privacy concerns about a Musk-backed voter information site and its collection of visitors’ personal data.

- [The AI Doc: Or How I Became an Apocaloptimist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_AI_Doc:_Or_How_I_Became_an_Apocaloptimist)
  - A documentary exploring the possible benefits and risks of artificial intelligence.

- The 2024 Columbus Ransomware Breach: Official Misinformation and Whistleblower Retaliation
  - ## ⚠️ The Initial Cover-Up & Inaccurate Claims
  * [NBC4i Investigative Report](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/investigates/mayor-ginther-shares-impact-of-columbus-data-leak-as-next-security-steps-begin/) — Details how Mayor Ginther's initial claims that the stolen database was "encrypted or corrupted" were immediately proven false.
  * [StateScoop Timeline Analysis](https://statescoop.com/columbus-ohio-ransomware-saga-legal-gray-areas-2024/) — Breaks down the "messy ransomware saga" timeline, documenting the press conferences where the administration mischaracterized forensic data.
  * [Ohio Capital Journal Commentary](https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2024/09/03/columbus-officials-embarrass-themselves-with-lack-of-transparency-on-data-attack/) — A sharp critique focusing on the lack of transparency, political deflection, and the immediate attempt to shift blame to the messenger.
  * [WOSU Public Media Retrospective](https://www.wosu.org/2025-07-18/one-year-later-little-details-or-accountability-shown-by-columbus-officials-after-ransomware-attack) — Highlights the structural fallout and the long-term lack of official accountability regarding the false statements made to the public.
  * ## 🛑 Retaliation & Defamation Against Whistleblower "Connor Goodwolf"
  * [CNBC National Investigative Report](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/09/15/dark-web-expert-warned-us-hometown-about-big-hack-the-city-is-suing.html) — A deep dive into how the city's legal strategy shocked cybersecurity experts and the framing of David Leroy Ross Jr. (Connor Goodwolf) as an active threat rather than a source of public verification.
  * [Ars Technica Legal Breakdown](https://arstechnica.com/security/2024/08/city-of-columbus-sues-man-after-he-discloses-severity-of-ransomware-attack/) — Outlines how the City of Columbus sued Ross for damages over $25,000, heavily mischaracterizing standard dark-web verification as a "sophisticated hacker operation".
  * [Malwarebytes Industry Response](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2024/09/city-of-columbus-tries-to-silence-security-researcher) — Documents the widespread pushback from global cybersecurity firms criticizing the city for trying to legally bully a research consultant who was merely checking their math.
  * [Freedom of the Press Foundation First Amendment Analysis](https://freedom.press/digisec/blog/city-sues-security-researcher-after-revelations-about-ransomware-attack/) — Focuses on the chilling effect that the city's lawsuit threatened to create for local journalists and tech researchers seeking truth in public data security.
  * ## ⚖️ The Dismissal of the Lawsuit
  * [WOSU Public Media Resolution Coverage](https://www.wosu.org/politics-government/2024-10-25/columbus-city-attorney-drops-lawsuit-against-cybersecurity-whistleblower) — Confirms that City Attorney Zach Klein formally dismissed the lawsuit with prejudice. It outlines the final agreement, which protected Ross's free speech rights to discuss the structural issues of the hack while safeguarding private data from further public distribution.
