## Books

### [The Computer Music Tutorial — Curtis Roads](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262361545/the-computer-music-tutorial/)

<figure>
  <a href="https://robotics.ucsb.edu/"><img src="https://www.robotics.ucsb.edu/uploads/Computer_Music_Tutorial_CR_700x700.jpg" alt="The Computer Music Tutorial Cover" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block; max-width:100%; width:auto; height:auto; max-height:240px;"></a>
  <figcaption><small><a href="https://robotics.ucsb.edu/">Image source</a></small></figcaption>
</figure>

An encyclopedic guide to computer music, covering digital audio, synthesis, signal processing, MIDI, algorithmic composition, psychoacoustics, and related tools. The linked MIT Press page is for the substantially updated second edition.

### [On the Sensations of Tone as a Physiological Basis for the Theory of Music — Hermann von Helmholtz](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/on-the-sensations-of-tone-as-a-physiological-basis-for-the-theory-of-music/6B5630E5D439286A8946CEB9D62BE6EC)

<figure>
  <a href="https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/128031586-on-the-sensations-of-tone-as-a-physiological-basis-for-the-theory-of-m"><img src="https://m.media-amazon.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1695768278i/128031586.jpg" alt="On the Sensations of Tone Cover" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block; max-width:100%; width:auto; height:auto; max-height:240px;"></a>
  <figcaption><small><a href="https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/128031586-on-the-sensations-of-tone-as-a-physiological-basis-for-the-theory-of-m">Image source</a></small></figcaption>
</figure>

A foundational 1863 study connecting acoustics, anatomy, hearing, consonance, and musical scales. Helmholtz’s work helped establish the scientific study of musical perception.

### [Electronic Projects for Musicians — Craig Anderton](https://craiganderton.org/electronic-projects-for-musicians-how-to-update-with-modern-parts/)

<figure>
  <a href="https://midiguy.com/index.php?main_page=product_info&amp;products_id=126"><img src="https://midiguy.com/images/Hal%20Leonard/ElectrProject.jpg" alt="Electronic Projects for Musicians Cover" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block; max-width:100%; width:auto; height:auto; max-height:240px;"></a>
  <figcaption><small><a href="https://midiguy.com/index.php?main_page=product_info&amp;products_id=126">Image source</a></small></figcaption>
</figure>

A practical introduction to building musician-oriented electronic devices such as effects and audio utilities. This author-maintained page adds guidance for substituting modern parts in the book’s vintage circuits.

### [The Theory and Technique of Electronic Music — Miller S. Puckette](https://msp.ucsd.edu/techniques/v0.09/book-html/)

<figure>
  <a href="https://msp.ucsd.edu/techniques.htm"><img src="https://msp.ucsd.edu/techniques/cover1.jpg" alt="Book cover" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block; max-width:100%; width:auto; height:auto; max-height:240px;"></a>
  <figcaption><small><a href="https://msp.ucsd.edu/techniques.htm">Image source</a></small></figcaption>
</figure>

A free online textbook that combines the mathematics of digital sound with hands-on synthesis and signal-processing examples in Pure Data. Topics include oscillators, sampling, filters, modulation, spectra, and spatialization.

### [The Cuckoo’s Egg — Clifford Stoll](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Cuckoos-Egg/Cliff-Stoll/9781668048160)

<figure>
  <a href="https://www.target.com/p/-/A-77098991"><img src="https://target.scene7.com/is/image/Target/GUEST_b62ccc78-bfa7-46b4-8105-9e6dfdf3aae5" alt="The Cuckoo’s Egg Paperback Cover" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block; max-width:100%; width:auto; height:auto; max-height:240px;"></a>
  <figcaption><small><a href="https://www.target.com/p/-/A-77098991">Image source</a></small></figcaption>
</figure>

Stoll’s first-person account of tracing a 75-cent accounting discrepancy to an international computer-espionage operation. It reads like a detective story while documenting an early landmark in network security.

### [Masters of Deception — Michelle Slatalla and Joshua Quittner](https://books.google.com/books?id=Xl-azgEACAAJ)

<figure>
  <a href="https://www.bol.com/nl/nl/p/masters-of-deception/9200000038696296/"><img src="https://media.s-bol.com/3O6lJxmZ6yY4/xvJ7Vgz/550x839.jpg" alt="Masters of Deception Book Cover" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block; max-width:100%; width:auto; height:auto; max-height:240px;"></a>
  <figcaption><small><a href="https://www.bol.com/nl/nl/p/masters-of-deception/9200000038696296/">Image source</a></small></figcaption>
</figure>

A nonfiction history of New York hacker culture and the conflict between the Masters of Deception and Legion of Doom. It follows teenage hackers, telephone networks, law enforcement, and the early social world of cyberspace.

## Food

### [Hominy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hominy)

<figure>
  <a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AHominy_%28maize%29.JPG"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Hominy_%28maize%29.JPG/1920px-Hominy_%28maize%29.JPG?_=20231115023724" alt="Hominy (Nixtamalized Maize)" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style="display:block; max-width:100%; width:auto; height:auto; max-height:240px;"></a>

</figure>

Dried maize treated with alkali through nixtamalization, which softens the kernels, improves flavor and nutrition, and makes the grain easier to process. Hominy is eaten whole or ground into masa and grits.

</figure>

A traditional Mexican soup or stew built around nixtamalized hominy, usually with meat or vegetables and garnishes such as cabbage, radish, onion, chile, and lime. Major regional styles include red, green, and white pozole.
