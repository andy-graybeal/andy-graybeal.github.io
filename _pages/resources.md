# Cybersecurity Resources

A curated collection of cybersecurity ethics, law, privacy, and governance references; certification and guided-learning materials; educators and video channels; security and analysis tools; and hands-on labs, practice environments, and CTF challenges.

> Use security-testing and password-auditing resources only with systems, accounts, and data you own or have explicit permission to test. Some resources provide real malware samples; treat all downloaded files as potentially dangerous and handle them only in an appropriately isolated environment.

## Ethics, Law, Privacy, and Governance

These resources provide useful professional and educational references; they are not a substitute for legal advice.

### Professional Codes of Ethics

- [ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct](https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics) — Ethical principles and professional responsibilities for computing practitioners, educators, students, and technology leaders.
- [ISC2 Code of Ethics](https://www.isc2.org/ethics) — Mandatory ethical canons and professional-conduct guidance for ISC2 members and certification holders.
- [ISACA Code of Professional Ethics](https://www.isaca.org/code-of-professional-ethics) — Professional expectations concerning governance, audit, control, security, risk management, privacy, and competent service.
- [IEEE Code of Ethics](https://www.ieee.org/about/corporate/governance/p7-8.html) — Ethical commitments for engineers and technology professionals concerning public welfare, honesty, competence, fairness, and responsible conduct.

### Cyberlaw and Responsible Disclosure

- [DOJ — Computer Fraud and Abuse Act Guidance (Justice Manual 9-48.000)](https://www.justice.gov/jm/jm-9-48000-computer-fraud) — Department of Justice charging and prosecution guidance for the CFAA, including considerations for good-faith security research.
- [DOJ — Electronic Communications Privacy Act of 1986](https://bja.ojp.gov/program/it/privacy-civil-liberties/authorities/statutes/1285) — Overview of the federal law governing interception of communications and access to stored electronic communications.
- [Stored Communications Act: Overview of Governmental Action](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/LSB10801) — Congressional Research Service overview of governmental access to stored communications under the SCA.
- [CISA Vulnerability Disclosure Policy Template](https://www.cisa.gov/vulnerability-disclosure-policy-template) — Template for defining authorized security research, reporting procedures, scope, expectations, and safe-harbor commitments.

### NIST Frameworks and Technical Guidance

- [NIST SP 800-86 — Guide to Integrating Forensic Techniques into Incident Response](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/86/final) — Guidance for incorporating computer and network forensics into incident-response processes.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — Voluntary framework for managing risks to individuals, organizations, and society arising from artificial-intelligence systems.
  - [NIST AI RMF 1.0 Explainer Video](https://www.nist.gov/video/introduction-nist-ai-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10-explainer-video) — Official introduction to the purpose and structure of the AI RMF.
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf) — Risk-management framework organized around the Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover functions.
- [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework) — Voluntary framework for identifying and managing privacy risk through enterprise risk-management processes.
- [NIST SP 800-63-4 — Digital Identity Guidelines](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/63/4/final) — Requirements and recommendations for identity proofing, authentication, federation, security, and privacy.
- [NIST Face Technology Evaluations (FRTE/FATE; formerly FRVT)](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/face-technology-evaluations-frtefate) — Independent evaluations of face-recognition and face-analysis technologies, including accuracy and demographic-performance research.

### Security Testing Frameworks and Methodologies

- [OWASP Web Security Testing Guide](https://owasp.org/www-project-web-security-testing-guide/latest/) — Structured guidance for planning, performing, and reporting web-application security tests.
- [Penetration Testing Execution Standard (PTES)](https://www.pentest-standard.org/index.php/Main_Page) — Penetration-testing lifecycle covering pre-engagement, intelligence gathering, threat modeling, vulnerability analysis, exploitation, post-exploitation, and reporting.
- [NIST SP 800-115 — Technical Guide to Information Security Testing and Assessment](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final) — Guidance for planning assessments, reviewing systems, discovering targets, identifying vulnerabilities, validating findings, and reporting results.
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) — Knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, procedures, software, groups, mitigations, and defensive data sources.
- [Lockheed Martin Cyber Kill Chain](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/capabilities/cyber/cyber-kill-chain.html) — Model describing seven stages of a targeted intrusion, from reconnaissance through actions on objectives.

### Privacy and Education

- [FTC Privacy and Security Resources](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) — Business guidance concerning data security, privacy practices, breach response, and compliance with consumer-protection law.
- [FERPA Overview](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa) — U.S. Department of Education overview of student and parent rights under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act.

#### Defensive Security Analysis

*Suggested progression: Security+ → CySA+ → BTL1 → CISSP after gaining qualifying experience*

- [CompTIA CySA+](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/cybersecurity-analyst/) — Analyst-focused certification covering security operations, vulnerability management, incident response, reporting, and communication.
  - [Pluralsight — CompTIA CySA+ (CS0-004)](https://www.pluralsight.com/paths/comptia-cysa-cs0-004) — Current-version video courses and exam-preparation material; subscription required after any trial period.
  - [LinkedIn Learning — CompTIA CySA+ (CS0-004) Cert Prep](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/comptia-cybersecurity-analyst-cysa-plus-cs0-004-cert-prep) — Current-version video course covering all four examination domains; subscription or eligible library access may be required.
- [Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1)](https://www.centri.org/certifications/blue-team-level-1) — Practical junior-level defensive certification covering incident response, SIEM investigations, digital forensics, phishing analysis, threat intelligence, and network-traffic analysis.

#### Systems and Security Operations

*Suggested progression: Security+ → SSCP → CISSP after gaining qualifying experience*

- [ISC2 Systems Security Certified Practitioner (SSCP)](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/sscp) — Security-administration and operations certification focused on implementing, monitoring, and maintaining secure infrastructure.
  - [Current SSCP Exam Outline](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/sscp/sscp-certification-exam-outline) — Official examination domains, objectives, weights, and experience requirements.
  - [SSCP Self-Study Resources](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/sscp/sscp-self-study-resources) — Official training links, flash cards, study tools, and preparation guidance.

#### Offensive Security and Penetration Testing

*Suggested progression: Security+ → PenTest+ or eJPT → PNPT or CPTS → OSCP+*

- [CompTIA PenTest+](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/pentest/) — Vendor-neutral penetration-testing certification covering engagement management, reconnaissance, vulnerability discovery, exploitation, post-exploitation, and reporting. Study for the current PT0-003 exam.
- [INE Junior Penetration Tester (eJPT)](https://security.ine.com/certifications/ejpt-certification/) — Hands-on, entry-level alternative to PenTest+ covering assessment methodology, network and host testing, exploitation, and web-application testing.
- [TCM Security Practical Network Penetration Tester (PNPT)](https://certifications.tcm-sec.com/pnpt/) — Practical professional-level examination involving OSINT, network and Active Directory testing, a written report, and a live findings presentation.
- [HTB Certified Penetration Testing Specialist (CPTS)](https://academy.hackthebox.com/preview/certifications/htb-certified-penetration-testing-specialist) — Practical penetration-testing certification supported by Hack The Box Academy’s Penetration Tester path.
- [Hack The Box Academy — Penetration Tester Path](https://academy.hackthebox.com/path/preview/penetration-tester) — Hands-on curriculum covering the complete penetration-testing process and preparation for CPTS.

- [OffSec OSCP+ Preparation](https://www.offsec.com/courses/pen-200/) — PEN-200: Penetration Testing with Kali Linux is OffSec’s official, hands-on training course for the OSCP+ certification.
  - [PEN-200 Course Syllabus](https://manage.offsec.com/app/uploads/2026/03/PEN-200_Syllabus.pdf) — Current topics, modules, exercises, and learning objectives.
  - [PEN-200 Learner Onboarding Guide](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406841351316-PEN-200-Onboarding-A-Learner-Introduction-Guide-to-the-OSCP) — Official guidance for approaching the course and labs.
  - [PEN-200 24-Week Learning Plan](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/19289813086612) — A structured study schedule from OffSec.
  - [OffSec Proving Grounds](https://www.offsec.com/products/proving-grounds/) — Practice environments including free Linux machines and paid labs designed to supplement OSCP+ preparation.

## Tools and Reference Sites

The tools below are arranged broadly in assessment-lifecycle order: intelligence gathering, discovery, vulnerability analysis, specialized technical assessment, exploitation, detection, analysis, and incident response. Framework tags identify direct relationships rather than implying that every tool belongs to every framework.

### Intelligence Gathering: OSINT and Passive Reconnaissance

**Framework alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering · NIST SP 800-115 — Target Identification and Network Discovery · Cyber Kill Chain — Reconnaissance

- [Malfrat’s OSINT Map](https://map.malfrats.industries/) — Maintained visual directory of tools and resources for open-source intelligence investigations.
- [OSINT Framework](https://osintframework.com/) — Influential visual directory of OSINT resources; reportedly no longer actively maintained, but retained here for reference and comparison.
- [Robtex](https://www.robtex.com/) — DNS, IP address, autonomous system, WHOIS, passive DNS, and network-infrastructure research tools.

### Intelligence Gathering: Active Reconnaissance and Network Discovery

#### [Nmap](https://nmap.org/)

Open-source network discovery, port scanning, service identification, operating-system detection, and security-auditing tool.

**Alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering and Vulnerability Analysis · NIST SP 800-115 — Network Discovery and Port/Service Identification · Cyber Kill Chain — Reconnaissance

- [Nmap Source — GitHub](https://github.com/nmap/nmap) — GitHub mirror of Nmap’s official source repository, including the Nmap Scripting Engine libraries and scripts.
- [Official Nmap Network Scanning Guide](https://nmap.org/book/) — Authoritative guide covering introductory through advanced Nmap usage; approximately half is available free online.
- [Quick Port Scanning Tutorial](https://nmap.org/book/port-scanning-tutorial.html) — Official walkthrough of common scans and how to interpret their results.

### Vulnerability Analysis and Security Assessment

#### [Tenable Nessus](https://www.tenable.com/products/nessus)

Commercial vulnerability-assessment platform for identifying software flaws, missing patches, insecure configurations, and other weaknesses across authorized systems.

**Alignment:** PTES — Vulnerability Analysis · NIST SP 800-115 — Vulnerability Scanning and Configuration Review

- [Tenable Nessus Learning Resources](https://www.tenable.com/education?id=3) — Official training for installing, configuring, and using Nessus.

- [Nessus Fundamentals Course](https://www.tenable.com/education/courses/nessus-fundamentals) — Paid course with targeted videos and demonstrations.

#### [Greenbone Community Edition / OpenVAS](https://greenbone.github.io/docs/latest/)

**Alignment:** PTES — Vulnerability Analysis · NIST SP 800-115 — Vulnerability Scanning and Configuration Review

- [Install with Greenbone Community Containers](https://greenbone.github.io/docs/latest/22.4/container/) — Guided installation using Docker or Podman containers.
- [Install on Kali Linux](https://greenbone.github.io/docs/latest/22.4/kali/index.html) — Distribution-specific installation and setup guide.
- [Perform Your First OpenVAS Scan](https://community.greenbone.net/blog/open-source-greenbone-vulnerability-scanner-first-scan/) — Community video walkthrough covering login, the scan wizard, and basic report results.

### Technical Assessment: Wireless Security

Wireless assessment generally requires a compatible Wi-Fi adapter whose Linux driver supports monitor mode; active testing and packet injection must be limited to networks and devices you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.

#### [Aircrack-ng](https://www.aircrack-ng.org/)

Open-source suite for auditing Wi-Fi networks. Its tools cover monitor-mode interface management, 802.11 packet capture, access-point and client discovery, packet injection testing, and authorized WPA/WPA2 key auditing.

**Alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering, Vulnerability Analysis, and Exploitation · NIST SP 800-115 — Wireless Scanning, Network Sniffing, and Password Cracking

- [Aircrack-ng Documentation](https://www.aircrack-ng.org/documentation.html) — Official documentation, individual tool references, tutorials, and troubleshooting material.
- [Aircrack-ng — GitHub](https://github.com/aircrack-ng/aircrack-ng) — Official source, build instructions, releases, manual pages, and issue tracker.

#### [Kismet](https://www.kismetwireless.net/)

Open-source passive wireless detector, packet-capture platform, wardriving tool, and wireless intrusion-detection system. In addition to Wi-Fi, it can support Bluetooth and selected software-defined-radio data sources.

**Alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering · NIST SP 800-115 — Wireless Scanning and Network Sniffing · Cyber Kill Chain — Reconnaissance

- [Kismet Documentation](https://www.kismetwireless.net/docs/) — Official installation, data-source, capture, logging, alerting, wardriving, and web-interface guidance.
- [Kismet — GitHub](https://github.com/kismetwireless/kismet) — Official GitHub mirror of the project source.

#### [hcxdumptool and hcxtools](https://github.com/ZerBea/hcxdumptool)

Linux tools for capturing and processing authorized WPA/WPA2/WPA3 assessment data. `hcxdumptool` writes modern `.pcapng` captures, while utilities such as `hcxpcapngtool` can prepare supported material for analysis with tools including Hashcat.

**Alignment:** PTES — Vulnerability Analysis and Exploitation · NIST SP 800-115 — Network Sniffing, Password Cracking, and Penetration Testing

- [hcxdumptool — GitHub](https://github.com/ZerBea/hcxdumptool) — Official source, hardware requirements, build instructions, operating guidance, and safety considerations.
- [hcxtools — GitHub](https://github.com/ZerBea/hcxtools) — Companion conversion, filtering, and analysis utilities for wireless capture files.

#### [bettercap](https://www.bettercap.org/)

Open-source network reconnaissance and assessment framework supporting Wi-Fi, Bluetooth Low Energy, Ethernet, IPv4/IPv6, HID, and other interfaces. It includes wireless discovery, packet capture, programmable modules, and a web interface.

**Alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering and Exploitation · NIST SP 800-115 — Network Discovery, Sniffing, and Penetration Testing · MITRE ATT&CK — Adversary-in-the-Middle (T1557)

- [bettercap Wi-Fi Documentation](https://www.bettercap.org/modules/wifi/) — Official wireless module commands, configuration options, requirements, and output formats.
- [bettercap — GitHub](https://github.com/bettercap/bettercap) — Official source, releases, installation guidance, modules, and issue tracker.

### Technical Assessment: Identity, Authentication, and File Services

These resources cover both legitimate administration and authorized security assessment of LDAP directories, Kerberos authentication, and SMB/Samba file and identity services.

#### [OpenLDAP Utilities](https://www.openldap.org/doc/admin25/guide.html)

OpenLDAP includes command-line utilities such as `ldapsearch` for querying directory entries, `ldapwhoami` for checking the authenticated identity, and `ldapadd`, `ldapmodify`, and `ldapdelete` for authorized directory administration.

**Alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering and Vulnerability Analysis · NIST SP 800-115 — Configuration Review and Account Examination

- [OpenLDAP Administrator’s Guide](https://www.openldap.org/doc/admin25/guide.html) — Official concepts, installation, configuration, access control, security, replication, maintenance, and troubleshooting guidance.
- [OpenLDAP Source](https://git.openldap.org/openldap/openldap) — Official project source repository; OpenLDAP hosts its source on its own Git service rather than GitHub.

#### [MIT Kerberos Tools](https://web.mit.edu/kerberos/krb5-latest/doc/)

The MIT Kerberos client and administrative tools include `kinit` for obtaining a ticket-granting ticket, `klist` for inspecting credential caches and keytabs, `kvno` for requesting service tickets, and `kdestroy` for removing cached credentials.

**Alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering and Vulnerability Analysis · NIST SP 800-115 — Configuration and Authentication Review · MITRE ATT&CK — Credential Access and Valid Accounts analysis

- [MIT Kerberos User Commands](https://web.mit.edu/Kerberos/www/krb5-latest/doc/user/user_commands/index.html) — Official references for ticket, password, configuration, and user-session commands.
- [MIT Kerberos — GitHub](https://github.com/krb5/krb5) — Official GitHub mirror of the MIT Kerberos source repository.

#### [Samba Administration and Client Tools](https://www.samba.org/samba/docs/)

The Samba suite includes `smbclient` for accessing and testing SMB shares, `rpcclient`, `nmblookup`, and `net` for service and identity operations, `samba-tool` for administration, and `testparm` for validating `smb.conf` configuration.

**Alignment:** PTES — Intelligence Gathering and Vulnerability Analysis · NIST SP 800-115 — Network Discovery and Configuration Review · MITRE ATT&CK — Network Service Discovery and Remote Services analysis

- [smbclient Manual](https://www.samba.org/samba/docs/current/man-html/smbclient.1.html) — Official command and option reference for connecting to SMB/CIFS services.
- [samba-tool Manual](https://www.samba.org/samba/docs/current/man-html/samba-tool.8.html) — Official reference for Samba identity, domain, DNS, group, user, policy, and service administration.
- [testparm Manual](https://www.samba.org/samba/samba/docs/man/manpages/testparm.1.html) — Official guidance for checking the internal correctness of Samba configuration files.
- [Samba Source — GitLab](https://gitlab.com/samba-team/samba) — Official source mirror; the Samba project uses GitLab rather than GitHub.

#### [Impacket](https://github.com/fortra/impacket)

Open-source Python classes and example tools for working with network protocols including SMB, Kerberos, LDAP, MS-RPC, NTLM, and others. It is widely used for protocol research, interoperability testing, CTFs, and authorized security assessments.

### Analysis: Debugging and Reverse Engineering

#### [WinDbg](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/)

Microsoft’s Windows debugger for analyzing crash dumps and debugging live user-mode programs, kernel-mode code, and drivers. Common inputs include `.dmp` crash-dump files, matching `.exe`, `.dll`, or `.sys` modules, and `.pdb` symbol files. Microsoft’s public symbol server supplies public Windows symbols automatically; symbols for third-party or privately built software must come from that software’s developer or build process.

**Alignment:** NIST SP 800-86 — Forensic and Malware Analysis · MITRE ATT&CK — analysis of Execution, Persistence, Privilege Escalation, and Defense Evasion behaviors · Cyber Kill Chain — Exploitation and Installation analysis

- [Install WinDbg](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/) — Official download, installation, requirements, and update instructions.
- [Getting Started with WinDbg](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/getting-started-with-windbg) — Introductory user-mode debugging exercise explaining breakpoints, modules, symbols, and commands.
- [Symbols and Symbol Files](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/symbols-and-symbol-files) — Explanation of Program Database (`.pdb`) files and why symbols must match the exact binary being examined.
- [Microsoft Public Symbol Server](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/microsoft-public-symbols) — Official service for downloading and locally caching matching Windows symbols.
- [Windows Symbol Packages and Their Deprecation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/debugger-download-symbols) — Microsoft formerly published downloadable offline symbol packages as MSI files but discontinued them in favor of its online symbol server. This history can matter when analyzing an older Windows build that requires an exact matching PDB.
- [Configure the WinDbg Symbol Path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/symbol-path) — Symbol-path syntax and cache configuration. A typical setup uses `.symfix C:\Symbols` followed by `.reload`.
- [WinDbg Samples — GitHub](https://github.com/microsoft/WinDbg-Samples) — Microsoft’s public debugger-extension, scripting, Time Travel Debugging, and API examples; this is a samples repository, not WinDbg’s core source code.

### Response: Digital Forensics and Incident Response

Forensic tools commonly work with raw disk images (`.dd`, `.raw`, or `.img`), Expert Witness images (`.E01`), memory captures (`.raw`, `.mem`, or `.dmp`), and collected operating-system artifacts such as Windows Event Logs (`.evtx`), Registry hives, the NTFS Master File Table (`$MFT`), Prefetch files, and browser databases. Work only with evidence that you are authorized to examine, preserve the original, and verify working copies with cryptographic hashes.

#### [Autopsy and The Sleuth Kit](https://www.autopsy.com/)

Open-source disk-forensics platform for examining file systems and storage images, recovering deleted files, searching content, building timelines, and organizing findings. Autopsy provides the graphical interface; The Sleuth Kit supplies the underlying forensic libraries and command-line tools.

**Alignment:** NIST SP 800-86 — Collection, Examination, Analysis, and Reporting · NIST SP 800-115 — Post-Testing Analysis and Reporting · MITRE ATT&CK — incident investigation and technique reconstruction
